Usage

Rules

Gradient

Only on the wordmark and on hero type. Never on body copy, never on backgrounds behind the logo — it kills the contrast the mark needs.

Backgrounds

Charcoal for premium and editorial. Copper Deep for full-bleed statement frames with the white mark. White for documents and print.

Mono lockup

Grey Mid on light grounds, Grey Light on dark. One flat value — no gradient in mono, no drop shadows, no outlines.

Clean-up

A stray #005BA5 object sits on page 8 of the source PDF. It's an Illustrator leftover, not a brand color — delete before release.
